# 生駒高原
## A famous spot for flower viewing at the foot of the majestic Kirishima mountain range
This plateau located at an altitude of 540 meters offers a panoramic view of Kobayashi City. The roughly 90,000 square meter grounds, set against a backdrop of the Kirishima mountain range, is filled with flower gardens where flowers bloom throughout the seasons, attracting countless visitors as one of the most famous and popular places to view flowers in Miyazaki prefecture.
